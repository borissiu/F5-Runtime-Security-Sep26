<h1>F5 AI Guardrails<a class="headerlink" href="#f5-ai-guardrails" title="Permalink to this heading">¶</a></h1>
<p><strong>F5 AI Guardrails</strong> is an enterprise-grade AI runtime security and governance solution developed by F5, Inc. that helps organizations protect, control, and monitor how their AI systems behave in real-world usage. It acts as a layer of safeguards around generative AI and AI agents — ensuring security, compliance, and responsible operation beyond what is built into the AI models themselves.</p>
<p>The focus of this module is to understand the deployment options and how to operate the F5 AI Guardrails console.</p>
